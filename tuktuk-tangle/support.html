TukTuk Tangle Support

Need help? Email mail@shubhsandes.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, the level number and what happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap a ride whose way out is clear: it drives out of the lot to a free pickup bay, and riders in its colour climb aboard in queue order. A full ride heads home. Friends wearing marigold garlands ride together and need two seats in the same ride. If every bay is taken and the rider at the front can't board, the level is lost, so plan the order.

What do Hints and Express Bays do?

A Hint shows a ride that is safe to tap now. An Express Bay opens an extra pickup bay for the level. You start with a few and get more from daily gifts and packs; when you run out, spend coins or watch a short video for one.

I can't tell some colours apart.

Turn on Settings → Route symbols: every colour gets its own symbol on the ride's roof and on the riders' caps.

What is the Mela Club?

An optional weekly, monthly or yearly membership: no ads between levels and no banner, a daily gift (200 coins, a Hint and an Express Bay), Hints and Express Bays without videos, and double coins from the daily gift calendar without a video. Open it from the crown on the home screen or from the Shop.

How do I cancel the Mela Club?

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TukTuk Tangle. You keep the membership until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought something but don't have it.

Tap Restore Purchases in the Shop or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account. If it still doesn't appear, email us the date of the purchase.

Are there ads?

The game is free with ads: optional reward videos, a full-screen ad now and then between levels from level 11 and a banner from level 20. Remove Ads turns off the full-screen ads and the banner for good; Mela Club members don't see them either.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. It is not stored online, so deleting the game deletes your progress. Remove Ads and an active Mela Club come back with Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Request it from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
