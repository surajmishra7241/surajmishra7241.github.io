TukTuk Tangle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy covers the game TukTuk Tangle (“the game”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”), on iPhone and iPad.

In short: the game is free. It shows ads (optional reward videos, a full-screen ad now and then between levels and a small banner on later levels), served through Unity LevelPlay by ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, and offers optional in-app purchases and an optional Mela Club subscription, all processed by Apple. There are no accounts and no server of ours: your progress stays on your device. With usage stats on (the default; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland they stay off) gameplay statistics, not linked to your name or account, go to Google Analytics for Firebase; switch them off any time in Settings → Usage stats. Ads are personalised only if usage stats are on and you allow tracking when Apple's prompt asks; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland ads are never personalised and the game does not ask.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels won, coins, Hints, Express Bays, daily gifts and missions), items you bought, your membership status, when you last saw an ad (so the game can space ads out) and your settings are saved in the game's own storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them; Remove Ads and an active Mela Club come back with Restore Purchases.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see how the game is played, improve it and measure our own advertising, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. When usage stats are on it receives:

  • an app-instance identifier created by Firebase and the identifier for vendors (IDFV), not your name, email or Apple ID;
  • device and app information: device model, iOS version, app version, language, country/region (derived from your IP address, which Google does not store as such), first-open and session times;
  • game events, for example: levels started, won and lost, Hints and Express Bays used, the home screen and Shop opened, daily gifts and missions claimed, purchases and subscriptions (product and price), reward videos watched, ads shown and their estimated value, and your answer to Apple's tracking prompt.

Google processes this under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and keeps detailed event data for 2 months (reports use aggregated counts).

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: nothing is collected unless you agree on Google's consent form; while the game does not show that form, nothing is collected there at all. The game treats you as there if your device's region is set to one of these countries.
  • Everywhere: turn it off any time in Settings → Usage stats. Stats then stop, ads are no longer personalised and the game does not ask for tracking.

Advertising

The game shows optional reward videos you choose to watch (an Express Bay, a Hint, double coins after a win, free coins in the Shop or a bigger daily gift), a full-screen ad now and then after you win a level (never while you are playing a level, never after a lost level, not in the first ten levels or your first minutes of play, and at most one every 90 seconds), and a small banner at the bottom of the play screen from level 20. Remove Ads turns the full-screen ads and the banner off for good; Mela Club members see neither and get video rewards without watching a video. Ad requests are not marked as directed at children.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies (ironSource), which picks each ad from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads; LevelPlay includes Unity's Ad Quality component, which checks the ads shown for quality and safety. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, these ad partners may collect from your device: IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, the identifier for vendors (IDFV), ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostics. Unity processes this under its own policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. When usage stats are on, the game also sends the estimated value of each ad shown (no personal data) to Google Analytics for Firebase.

  • Tracking. If usage stats are on, after your first few levels the game shows a short note and then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, once. Only if you choose Allow do the ad partners receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), may ads be personalised to your interests, and may Google Analytics share statistics with Google's advertising services to measure which of our ads brought you to the game. Either answer leaves the game the same, with the same number of ads; change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: only non-personalised ads are requested and the game does not ask for tracking. The game treats you as there if your device's region is set to one of these countries.
  • US privacy laws: unless you allow tracking, the game tells the ad partners not to sell or share your information.

Purchases and the Mela Club subscription

Purchases (the Starter Pack, the Rush Hour Pack, coin packs and Remove Ads) and the Mela Club subscription are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. The App Store tells the game which items and which membership your Apple ID owns, with product and transaction identifiers and dates, so they can be delivered, renewed and restored; this stays on your device. With usage stats on, a new purchase's product, price and transaction identifier also go to Google Analytics for Firebase (see above). You manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Apple services

The game may ask Apple to show its standard “rate this app” prompt after a couple of good wins. That prompt is run by Apple; we receive no information from it. Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated download, sales and crash statistics through App Store Connect if you have chosen to share them with developers.

Contacting us

Settings → Support in the game opens our support page. If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you write, use it only to answer you and delete the thread within 12 months.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health data or browsing history, and we do not sell personal information. If you allow tracking, our ad partners may use your advertising identifier for personalised ads, which some US state laws treat as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising; stop this any time by turning tracking off in iOS Settings or usage stats off in the game.

Children

TukTuk Tangle is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at mail@shubhsandes.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Switch usage stats off in Settings → Usage stats, turn tracking off or reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings, or delete the game. We don't know who you are: the game has no account, and analytics data is tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to a name or account. To ask Google to delete it, email us the approximate date you played and your device model and we will request deletion of that app instance. For information the ad partners hold, use the choices in Unity's policy linked above, or email mail@shubhsandes.com and we will help; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
