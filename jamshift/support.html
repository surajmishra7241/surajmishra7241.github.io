Jam Shift Support

Need help? Email mail@shubhsandes.com or use Settings → Contact Support in the game. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your iPhone model and what happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Every vehicle moves only forwards and backwards along its bay. Drag the cars, buses and vans aside to clear the glowing lane, then drive the gold car out through the gate. Undo and Restart are always free, and every level has a solution.

What do the tools do?

Headlights show the next good move, Autopilot plays the next three moves for you and Tow lifts one vehicle out of the lot. They unlock on levels 3, 7 and 11.

What is the VIP Pass?

An optional monthly or yearly subscription: no ads at all, a VIP-only Night Jam every day, a daily gift and instant video rewards. The yearly plan may start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Open it from the Shop or Settings.

How do I cancel the VIP Pass?

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Jam Shift. You keep the subscription until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought something but don't have it.

Tap Restore Purchases in the Shop or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account. If it still doesn't appear, email us the date of the purchase.

Are there ads?

The game is free with ads from level 5: optional reward videos, a full-screen ad now and then after a won level and a banner on the play screen. No Ads turns off the full-screen ads and the banner for good; VIP Pass members see no ads at all.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device and in your iCloud (Settings → iCloud save). Reinstall the game or open it on another iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account and your levels, stars, coins and rides come back. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases.

Are there leaderboards?

Yes. Sign in to Game Center and tap Settings → Game Center for the leaderboards and achievements.

How do I get a refund?

Request it from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
