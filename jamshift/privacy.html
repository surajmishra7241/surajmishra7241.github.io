Jam Shift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy covers the game Jam Shift (“the game”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”), on iPhone and iPad.

In short: the game is free. It shows ads (optional reward videos, an occasional full-screen ad after a won level and a small banner while you play, from level 5 on), served through Unity LevelPlay by ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, and offers optional in-app purchases and an optional VIP Pass subscription, all processed by Apple. There are no accounts and no server of ours: your progress stays on your device and, if you leave iCloud save on, in your own iCloud. With usage stats on (the default) anonymous gameplay statistics go to Google Analytics for Firebase; switch them off any time in Settings → Share usage stats. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland no statistics are collected at all. Ads are personalised only if usage stats are on and you allow tracking when Apple's prompt asks; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland ads are never personalised.

Information stored on your device and in your iCloud

Your progress (levels solved, stars, coins, tools, rides in your garage, daily gifts and missions), items you bought, your VIP Pass status and your settings are saved in the game's own storage on your device. With Settings → iCloud save on (the default) a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your progress comes back if you reinstall the game or play on another iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple stores this copy under its own privacy policy; we cannot see it. Turn iCloud save off in the game, or remove the game's iCloud data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends your number of levels solved and stars to its leaderboards and unlocks achievements. Apple runs Game Center and shows your Game Center nickname to other players as set in your Game Center settings; we do not receive your Apple ID or contacts. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see how the game is played, improve it and measure our own advertising, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. When usage stats are on it receives:

  • an app-instance identifier created by Firebase and the identifier for vendors (IDFV), not your name, email or Apple ID;
  • device and app information: device model, iOS version, app version, language, country/region (derived from your IP address, which Google does not store as such), first-open and session times;
  • game events: levels started, won and left, tools used, screens and offers viewed, purchases and subscriptions (product and price), reward videos watched and the estimated value of ads shown.

Google processes this under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and keeps detailed event data for 2 months (reports use aggregated counts).

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: nothing is collected. The game treats you as there if your device's region is one of these countries.
  • Everywhere: turn it off any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Stats then stop, ads are no longer personalised and the game does not ask for tracking.

Advertising

The game shows optional reward videos you choose to watch (for example double coins, a free tool or free coins), a full-screen ad now and then as you leave a won level (never while you are solving one, and not on the first four levels), and a small banner at the bottom of the play screen from level 5 on. No Ads turns the full-screen ads and the banner off for good; VIP Pass members see no ads at all. Ad requests are not marked as directed at children.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies (ironSource), which picks each ad from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, these ad partners may collect from your device: IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, the identifier for vendors (IDFV), ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostics. They process this under Unity's policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, Unity Ads). When usage stats are on, the game also sends the estimated value of each ad shown (no personal data) to Google Analytics for Firebase.

  • Tracking. If usage stats are on, the game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt once, after level 3, with a short note first. Only if you choose Allow do the ad partners receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), may ads be personalised to your interests, and may Google Analytics share statistics with Google's advertising services to measure which of our ads brought you to the game. Either answer leaves the game the same; change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. With usage stats off the game never asks and ads are not personalised.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: only non-personalised ads are requested, whatever the other settings. The game treats you as there if your device's region is one of these countries.
  • US privacy laws: when ads are not personalised, the game also tells the ad partners not to sell or share your information.

Purchases and the VIP Pass subscription

Purchases (the Starter Kit, Driver's Kit, Royal Garage Chest, coin packs, No Ads and the No Ads Bundle) and the VIP Pass subscription are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. The App Store tells the game which items and which subscription your Apple ID owns, with product, transaction identifiers and dates, so they can be delivered, renewed and restored; this stays on your device. You manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Apple services

The game may ask Apple to show its standard “rate this app” prompt after a good win. That prompt is run by Apple; we receive no information from it. Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated download, sales and crash statistics through App Store Connect if you have chosen to share them with developers.

Contacting us

If you email support from Settings → Contact Support, the draft includes the game version, your current level, your device model and iOS version so we can help. We receive your email address and anything you write only if you send it, use it only to answer you and delete the thread within 12 months.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health data or browsing history, and we do not sell personal information. If you allow tracking, our ad partners may use your advertising identifier for personalised ads, which some US state laws treat as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising; stop this any time by turning tracking off in iOS Settings or usage stats off in the game.

Children

Jam Shift is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at mail@shubhsandes.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Switch usage stats off in Settings → Share usage stats, change tracking in iOS Settings, or delete the game. We don't know who you are: analytics data is tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to a name or account. To ask Google to delete it, email us the approximate date you played and your device model and we will request deletion of that app instance. For data the ad partners hold, use Unity's privacy request options in the policy linked above. Email mail@shubhsandes.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
