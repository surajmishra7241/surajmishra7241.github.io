Need help? Email mail@shubhsandes.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, the level number and what happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap a vessel to lift it, then tap another to pour. A potion can only pour onto the same colour or into an empty space. Fill every vessel with a single colour to win. Stuck? Use Undo, a Hint, or add an extra flask.

Can every level be solved?

Yes. Every one of the 1,000 levels was checked by a solver before release.

I find some colours hard to tell apart.

Turn on Settings → Colour symbols: each colour then carries its own symbol.

There is no sound.

Turn up the volume with the side buttons while the game is open, and check Settings → Sound and Music in the game.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. Deleting the game deletes its progress.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free. It offers optional reward videos, a small banner during play after the first levels and an occasional ad after a won level; No Ads turns the banner and those ads off. Purchases are optional and Restore Purchases is in Settings.

How do I cancel Arcane Circle?

Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Hueberry Lab and tap Cancel Subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Request it from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

With usage stats on, anonymous gameplay statistics go to Google Analytics for Firebase; switch them off in Settings → Share usage stats. Ads come from Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. See the Privacy Policy.
