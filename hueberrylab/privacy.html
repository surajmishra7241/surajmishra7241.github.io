Hueberry Lab: Potion Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers the game Hueberry Lab: Potion Sort (“the game”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”), on iPhone and iPad.

In short: the game is free with optional in-app purchases and shows ads (optional reward videos, a small banner during play after the first levels and an occasional full-screen ad after a won level), served through Unity LevelPlay by ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your device. With your permission (asked first in the EEA, UK and Switzerland; on by default elsewhere) anonymous gameplay statistics go to Google Analytics for Firebase; switch them off any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Ads are personalised only if usage stats are on and you allow tracking when Apple's prompt asks.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, daily rewards, vessels and collections), items you bought and your settings are saved in the game's own storage on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the game deletes it.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see how the game is played, improve its levels and measure our own advertising, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. When usage stats are on it receives:

  • an app-instance identifier created by Firebase and the identifier for vendors (IDFV), not your name, email or Apple ID;
  • device and app information: device model, iOS version, app version, language, country/region (derived from your IP address, which Google does not store as such), first-open and session times;
  • game events: tutorial steps, levels started and finished, boosters used, daily rewards, shop visits, purchases (product and price), reward videos watched, full-screen ads shown and the estimated value of ads shown.

Google processes this under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and keeps detailed event data for 2 months (the Google Analytics default; reports use aggregated counts).

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: nothing is collected until you choose Allow on the one-time privacy card. The game treats you as there if your device's region or time zone is in one of these countries.
  • Everywhere: turn it off any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Stats then stop and ads are no longer personalised.

Advertising

The game shows optional reward videos you choose to watch (for example, for free coins, an extra life, a booster, an extra flask or a second spin of the lucky wheel), a small banner during play after the first levels, and, after your first levels, an occasional full-screen ad as you leave a won level. No Ads turns the banner and full-screen ads off for good; reward videos stay optional. The game is made for a general audience, so ad requests are not marked as directed at children.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies (ironSource), which picks each ad from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads; LevelPlay includes Unity's Ad Quality component, which checks the ads shown for quality and safety. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, these ad partners may collect from your device: IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, the identifier for vendors (IDFV), ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostics. They process this under Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. When usage stats are on, the game also sends the estimated value of each ad shown (no personal data) to Google Analytics for Firebase.

  • Tracking. If usage stats are on, the game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt once, after a few levels. Only if you choose Allow do the ad partners receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), may ads be personalised to your interests, and may Google Analytics share statistics with Google's advertising services to measure which of our ads brought you to the game. Either answer leaves the game the same; change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: if you choose No thanks on the privacy card (or later switch usage stats off), the game tells the ad partners not to personalise ads (and, for US privacy laws, not to sell or share your data) and does not show Apple's tracking prompt.

In-app purchases and the Arcane Circle subscription

Purchases (coin packs, bundles, No Ads and the Arcane Circle membership) are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The App Store tells the game which items your Apple ID owns so they can be delivered and restored (product, transaction identifiers and dates); this stays on your device. Arcane Circle renews automatically until you cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Restore Purchases is in the game's Settings.

Apple services

The game may ask Apple to show its standard “rate this app” prompt after you win levels. That prompt is run by Apple; we receive no information from it. Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated download and crash statistics through App Store Connect if you have chosen to share them with developers in your device settings.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health data or browsing history, and we do not sell personal information. If you allow tracking, our ad partners may use your advertising identifier for personalised ads, which some US state laws treat as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can stop this at any time by turning tracking off in iOS Settings or usage stats off in the game.

Children

Hueberry Lab is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at mail@shubhsandes.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Switch usage stats off in Settings → Share usage stats, change tracking in iOS Settings, or delete the game. We don't know who you are: analytics data is tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to a name or account. To ask Google to delete it, email us the approximate date you played and your device model and we will request deletion of that app instance. Email mail@shubhsandes.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
