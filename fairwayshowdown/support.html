Frequently asked questions

How do I hit a shot?

Drag to aim and set the distance (a faint blue line shows where the wind will take the ball), choose a club and spin, then hold the swing button and let go with the needle in the gold zone. The white centre is a flush strike. On the green, the drifting marks show the slope: aim above the hole on a break.

Which golfer is mine?

Yours is always marked in teal, with a YOU tag and a teal ring at its feet; your rival is always coral. The card at the top of the screen says whose shot it is.

How do I change my golfer's clothes?

Open Golfers, pick a golfer you own and tap Wardrobe. Clothes are bought with coins or gems earned by playing and change looks only, never stats.

There is no sound.

Turn up the volume with the side buttons while the game is open, and check Settings → Music and Sound effects in the game. The game plays even with the ring switch on silent.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. It is not stored online, so deleting the game deletes your progress.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. You buy the game once; there are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Request it from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
