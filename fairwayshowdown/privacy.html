Fairway Showdown — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers the game Fairway Showdown (“the game”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”), on iPhone.

In short: the game collects nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to any server of ours or anyone else's. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (golfers, clubs, clothes, coins and gems earned in play, tour results, trophies, records) and your settings are saved in a file inside the game's own storage on your device. We cannot see it. It is included in your device's own backups if you have them turned on, under Apple's terms. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or usage data. It contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or attribution, does not use the advertising identifier and never shows Apple's tracking permission prompt.

Apple services

The game may ask Apple to show its standard “rate this app” prompt after you win a championship. That prompt is run by Apple; we receive no information from it. Downloading and paying for the game is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated download and crash statistics through App Store Connect if you have chosen to share them with developers in your device settings.

Sharing a result

If you tap Share after a match, the game opens your device's share sheet with a short text of your result. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Children

Fairway Showdown is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To erase your progress, delete the game from your device. Questions are welcome at mail@shubhsandes.com.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting any data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details first.
