Need help? Email mail@shubhsandes.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your device model and what happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Match the top card by its family (Electric Violet star, Sunset Coral sun, Emerald Pulse diamond, Ocean Cyan wave) or by its number or action. Wild cards can always be played. Can't play? Tap the deck to draw. Be the first to empty your hand. The first match has a short guided tutorial, and How to Play on the home screen explains every card.

What are Star Powers?

Every character has a once-per-match Star Power (for example Riya's Encore Dance or Alex's Calculated). Each card you play in your character's signature family adds a charge; at three charges, tap the Star Power card on your turn.

What is the Final Spotlight?

When a player drops to one card, the others get a moment to Heckle them: spend one Star Power charge and they draw a card. A heckled player gets Encore Immunity until their next turn, so nobody can be heckled twice in a row.

How do I play with friends?

Choose Friends Night: two to four friends pass one iPhone or iPad around, and party guests can fill the table up to six seats. A curtain hides each hand between turns. No Wi-Fi needed.

How do I unlock venues?

Vegas After Dark and Bollywood Boulevard are open from the start. Win 3 matches for Hollywood Spotlight and 6 for Campus Carnival. Each venue has its own party event, music and look.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. If the game is closed during a match, you can pick it up where you left off.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. The game is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. Coins come only from playing and the free Daily Spin.

Can I turn off sounds or vibration?

Yes: Settings (the gear) has switches for sound effects, music, haptics, card hints and fast bots.

Does it need the internet?

No. Party Royale plays fully offline.
