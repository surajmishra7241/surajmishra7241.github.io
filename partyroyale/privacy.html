Party Royale — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 (covers version 1.1)

This policy explains how the game Party Royale (“the game”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the game on iPhone and iPad.

In short: there are no accounts, and your profile and progress stay on your device. The game is free and shows ads: reward videos you choose to watch and, after your first few matches, a full-screen ad between matches (never for Royale Edition owners). It measures how the game is played with Google Analytics for Firebase, only after you allow it in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, never for players under 13, and you can turn it off in Settings. Ads are personalised only if you are 16 or older and allow tracking. Purchases are handled by Apple. Feedback you choose to send reaches us without your name or any identifier.

Information stored on your device

The player name you type (up to 12 letters, it can be any nickname), the character you play as, your coins, XP, level, wins and streaks, the venues and cosmetic items you have unlocked and equipped, your daily goals, the date of your last free Daily Spin, an unfinished match so you can resume it, your house rules, your settings and your answers to the privacy questions below are saved in the game's own storage on your device. To make sure a purchase is delivered exactly once, the game also keeps the App Store transaction numbers of your purchases on the device. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it. If your device backs up apps (for example iCloud Backup), that backup is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot access it.

Your age

The first time you reach the Home screen, the game asks how old you are (under 13, 13 to 15, or 16 or older). The answer stays on your device (change it in Settings → Age) and decides:

  • Under 13: no analytics, never the tracking prompt, and every ad request is marked child-directed (COPPA), not personalised and limited to the “G” (general audience) content rating.
  • 13 to 15: analytics as described below; ads are not personalised (marked under the age of consent) and rated G.
  • 16 or older: analytics as described below; ads are rated G and personalised only if you allow tracking.

Nothing is measured and no ad is requested until you answer.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how many people play, which parts of the game they enjoy and which of our ads bring new players, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. When analytics is allowed it receives:

  • an app-instance identifier created by Firebase (not your name, email, Apple ID or the advertising identifier);
  • device and app information: device model, iOS version, app version, language, country/region (derived from your IP address, which Google does not store as such), first-open and session times;
  • game events: matches finished (win or loss and the coins earned), Spotlight Tour chapters started and finished, daily goals and Daily Spins claimed, coins earned and spent, characters, venues and cosmetic items viewed, tried or chosen, achievements, saved house rules, shop and product views, purchases (product and price) and restores, reward videos watched and the estimated value of ads shown, the feedback card being shown, skipped or sent (with the rating and reasons you ticked, never your note), Apple's review prompt, that you answered the age question (not your answer), and your answer to Apple's tracking prompt.

We use this to improve the game and to measure our own advertising campaigns (for example, how many installs an ad brought). Unless you allow tracking (below), it is not used for personalised advertising and is not combined with data from other companies' apps or websites. Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and keeps detailed event data for 2 months (the Google Analytics default; reports use aggregated counts).

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: nothing is collected until you choose Allow on the one-time privacy card.
  • Everywhere: turn it off any time in Settings → Share play stats. Analytics stops on that device.

Advertising

On iPhone and iPad the game shows reward videos you choose to watch (to double a match's coins, for bonus Daily Spins, for free coins in the shop, or to try an item for a match) and, after your first few matches, a full-screen ad as you leave a match's results. Royale Edition owners never see ads: their bonuses are instant Royale Perks. All ads are limited to the “G” content rating.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies (ironSource), which picks each ad from ironSource Ads, Google AdMob and Unity Ads. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, these ad partners may collect from your device: IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, the identifier for vendors (IDFV), ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostics. Each processes this under its own policy: Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, Unity Ads) and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. When analytics is on, the game also sends the estimated value of each ad shown (no personal data) to Google Analytics for Firebase.

  • Tracking. If you said you are 16 or older and analytics is on, the game explains and then shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt once, after your first finished match. Only if you choose Allow do the ad partners receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), may ads be personalised to your interests, and may Google Analytics share play statistics with Google's advertising services to measure which of our ads brought you to the game. Either answer leaves the game the same; change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players under 16 are never asked.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks for your ad choices before the first ad; change them later in Settings → Ad choices.

Feedback you choose to send

After your first finished match, and at any time from Settings, the game asks how you like it. If you tap Send, we receive: the rating you picked (1–5), any reasons you ticked (for example “Too hard”), the optional note you typed (up to 140 characters), the game version, the platform (iOS) and your device language. Nothing else is attached: no name, account, email, device identifier or advertising identifier, and we do not store your IP address with it. It travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our own server and is used only to improve the game. It is never sold, shared with advertisers or used for tracking.

Sending feedback is optional: you can close the card without answering. Please don't type personal details into the note. Feedback is kept for 12 months and then deleted automatically. To have something you sent deleted sooner, email us the date and what you wrote and we will delete it. If you choose Email us instead, see “Contacting support” below.

Reporting an ad. Settings → Feedback → Report an ad sends us only the reason you pick (for example “Not OK for kids”), the game version, the platform and your device language, in the same anonymous way as feedback, so we can ask the ad network to block it. Choosing “Something else” opens an email to us instead.

In-app purchases

The game offers the one-time Royale Edition and Spotlight Tour and packs of coins, bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. The App Store tells the game what was bought so it can be delivered, and Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated sales reports. The Royale Edition and the Spotlight Tour can be restored with Restore in Settings on any device signed in to the same Apple ID. Coins are consumable and are not restorable.

Game settings from our server

When it can, the game downloads a small settings file from our server (for example, to switch a feature off if it misbehaves, or to adjust how often ads appear). The request carries no information about you. Like any internet request it reveals your IP address to the server; it is not stored with anything else and appears only in short-lived technical logs that rotate automatically. The game works fully offline.

Sharing and invite links

Invite Friends and Share My Win open your device's share sheet with a picture and a link. You choose who to send it to; we never see your contacts or messages. When someone opens the link, our server counts the opening in a daily total per kind of link (for example “win”), with no information about the person. These totals are kept for 13 months.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you email us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health data or browsing history, and we do not sell personal information. If you allow tracking, our ad partners may use your advertising identifier for personalised ads, which some US state laws treat as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can stop this at any time by turning tracking off in iOS Settings.

Pass-and-play

Friends Night lets two to four friends share one device. Nothing about the other players is stored beyond the match itself.

Coins, XP and the Daily Spin

Coins are earned by playing, from daily goals, the free Daily Spin and optional reward videos, and can also be bought. They are only for cosmetic items in the game, have no cash value and cannot be sold, cashed out or exchanged for anything outside the game. The Daily Spin is free; it never costs coins or money. Watching an optional reward video can add bonus spins (up to 3 a day) or free coins (up to 5 times a day).

Apple

When you download or update the game, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example the number of downloads per country, and crash reports if you choose to share them with developers in your device's settings). This is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy and does not identify you.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Players who say they are under 13 get no analytics, are never asked about tracking, and only see child-directed, non-personalised ads rated G. Their feedback card has no note field (only the stars and reasons). The feedback card is anonymous; if you believe a child has sent us personal information (in a feedback note or by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time in Settings → Share play stats, change ad choices in Settings → Ad choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland) or tracking in iOS Settings, or delete the game. Analytics data is not linked to your identity, so to ask Google to delete it, email us the approximate date you played and your device model and we will request deletion of that app instance. Because feedback is anonymous we cannot link it to you, but we will delete any feedback or email you identify to us. Write to mail@shubhsandes.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released. Version 1.0 of the game does not connect to the internet at all; version 1.1 adds the feedback card, in-app purchases, sharing links, game settings from our server, ads and analytics, as described above.
