Party Royale — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Party Royale (“the game”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the game on iPhone and iPad.

In short: the game collects nothing. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and the game does not connect to the internet. Your profile and progress are saved only on your own device.

Information stored on your device

The player name you type (up to 12 letters, it can be any nickname), the character you play as, your coins, XP, level, wins and streaks, the venues you have unlocked, the date of your last free Daily Spin, an unfinished match so you can resume it, your house rules and your settings (sound, music, haptics, card hints, bot speed) are saved in the game's own storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the game deletes them. If your device backs up apps (for example iCloud Backup), that backup is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot access it.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers, the advertising identifier (IDFA), usage data, crash reports or purchase details. The game contains no third-party analytics or advertising code, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Pass-and-play

Friends Night lets two to four friends share one device. Nothing about the other players is stored beyond the match itself.

Coins, XP and the Daily Spin

Coins and XP are earned only by playing and from the free Daily Spin. They cannot be bought, sold, cashed out or exchanged for anything outside the game.

Apple

When you download or update the game, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example the number of downloads per country, and crash reports if you choose to share them with developers in your device's settings). This is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy and does not identify you.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

Children

The game is made for players aged 12 and over and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information. Because the game collects none, the only information we may hold is a support email you sent us; write to mail@shubhsandes.com and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
