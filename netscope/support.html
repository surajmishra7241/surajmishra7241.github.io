Common questions

Why does ping say “TCP connection” instead of ICMP?

Some networks block ICMP. NetScope then measures TCP connection time instead and tells you which method it used.

LAN discovery finds nothing

Allow Local Network access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → NetScope.

Does NetScope work as a VPN?

No. NetScope does not run a VPN or route other apps’ traffic. Its Proxy Lab safety-checks proxies you provide — exit address, HTTPS interception and speed for SOCKS5 and HTTP proxies; reachability and certificate checks for Shadowsocks, Trojan, VLESS and VMess — and exports your rules as a PAC file or Clash rules for the browser, router or proxy app that does the routing.

The Wi-Fi Safety Check says HTTPS is intercepted

A certificate installed on your device (often by a company, school or antivirus) lets something read encrypted traffic. Review Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and remove profiles you don't recognise.

Is my data uploaded?

No. See the privacy policy for exactly which connections each tool makes.
