NetScope — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers NetScope for iPhone, iPad and Mac (“the app”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”).

In short: NetScope collects nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no server of ours. Results stay on your device. The app only contacts the network when you run a tool — that is what a network tool does — and only the destinations listed below.

Network connections the app makes

Every connection is started by you. Nothing runs in the background.

DestinationWhen
Hosts, domains, IP addresses and URLs you enterWhen you run DNS, ping, traceroute, HTTP/TLS, port scan, TCP client, TLS, email or WHOIS tools
Devices on your local network, including your routerWhen you run LAN discovery or the Wi-Fi Safety Check (router: Telnet, FTP, TR-069 and web-admin ports only)
Public DNS resolvers: Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, OpenDNSWhen you choose one, run the resolver benchmark or DNS propagation
Cloudflare (1.1.1.1 trace endpoint)When you tap “Look Up” to see your public IP, and during a Proxy Safety Check (once directly and once through your proxy, to compare the two addresses)
Cloudflare speed endpointOnly when you run a speed test
rdap.org and the registries it points toWhen you run WHOIS / RDAP
www.apple.com, www.wikipedia.org, captive.apple.com, one.one.one.oneDuring the Wi-Fi Safety Check, to test HTTPS interception, DNS and captive portals
Proxy servers, subscription URLs and rule-set URLs you addWhen you import or test them. A Proxy Safety Check sends requests to one.one.one.one through the proxy you choose

Like any website, these services see your IP address when contacted. We receive nothing from them.

Information stored on your device

  • History, saved hosts, workflows, certificate watchlist, network snapshots, remembered Wi-Fi networks (a name you choose plus the router address, certificate roots, DNS answers and open router ports seen when you saved it), proxy profiles and rules are stored in the app's private storage. You can turn history off or clear it in Settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.
  • Proxy passwords and user IDs are stored only in the iOS/macOS Keychain on this device, are excluded from backups and device transfer, and are never included in exports, history or logs.
  • Certificate reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device.

Permissions

Local Network is requested the first time you scan your network. Notifications are requested only if you turn on certificate expiry reminders. You can change both in Settings.

Information we collect

None. The app contains no SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or attribution, does not use the advertising identifier and never shows Apple's tracking prompt. Its only other code is Apple's own open-source swift-certificates, swift-asn1 and swift-crypto libraries, used to decode certificates on the device; we check them against the public vulnerability database before every release. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

VPN and proxies

NetScope does not run a VPN and does not route other apps' traffic. It stores, imports and safety-checks proxy profiles you provide, and can export your rules as a PAC file or Clash rules for use in other software.

Apple services

Downloading and paying for the app is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics.

Children

The app is made for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Questions: mail@shubhsandes.com.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting any data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details first.
