StoragePilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers StoragePilot for iPhone, iPad and Mac (“the app”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”).

In short: StoragePilot collects nothing and cannot connect to the internet. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. All scanning, comparison and compression happens on your device.

What the app reads, and why

DataWhenWhat happens to it
Photos & videosOnly after you allow Photos access and start an analysisSmall thumbnails and file contents are read on the device to find exact duplicates, similar shots and large videos. Nothing leaves the device.
ContactsOnly after you allow Contacts access and open the Contacts cleanerNames, phone numbers and emails are compared on the device to find duplicates.
Files and folders (Mac, and Files on iPhone)Only folders you chooseFile names, sizes and dates are read to show what uses space. File contents are read only to confirm exact duplicates (a fingerprint is computed on the device).

What the app changes — only when you confirm

The app never deletes or changes anything automatically.

  • Photos and videos you choose are deleted only after you confirm, through Apple's own confirmation. They go to Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Video compression saves a new, smaller copy. The original is kept unless you delete it separately.
  • Merging contacts adds the phone numbers and emails from the duplicates to the contact you choose to keep, then deletes the duplicates — only after you review the group and tap Merge.
  • Files on Mac are moved to the Trash (so you can put them back) only after you confirm.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a local history of your cleanups (sizes, dates and, by default, file names only), Space Timeline snapshots (the sizes of folders inside the folders you scan, so the next scan can show what grew — never file contents) and your settings, inside its private storage. In Settings you can choose how long it is kept, whether full paths are recorded, and delete all of it at any time. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no network access: on Mac its sandbox does not allow internet connections at all. It contains no SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or attribution, does not use the advertising identifier and never shows Apple's tracking prompt. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Apple services

Downloading and paying for the app is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics.

Children

The app is made for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can withdraw Photos or Contacts access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security. Questions: mail@shubhsandes.com.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting any data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details first.
