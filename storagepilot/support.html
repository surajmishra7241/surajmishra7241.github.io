Common questions

Why can't it clear “System Data” on my iPhone?

iOS doesn't allow any App Store app to read or clear the system's or other apps' storage. StoragePilot helps with what apps can safely manage: your photos, videos, contacts and the files you choose.

I deleted a photo by mistake

Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted and tap Recover. Deleted photos stay there for 30 days.

I removed a file on my Mac by mistake

Open the Trash in the Dock, select the file and choose Put Back.

Why are some photos skipped?

Photos stored only in iCloud (with “Optimise iPhone Storage”) are skipped rather than downloaded, so the analysis never uses your mobile data. The app shows how many were skipped.

The Mac app can't see my Developer caches

Mac App Store apps are sandboxed. In Developer Cleanup, choose Grant Access and select your Home folder once; the app remembers this permission.

Does it upload anything?

No. StoragePilot has no internet access at all. See the privacy policy.
