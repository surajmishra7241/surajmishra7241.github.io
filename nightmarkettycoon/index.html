Night Market Tycoon

Turn one glowing street stall into the busiest night-market food empire in Saffron Bay.

Cook street noodles, tomato soup, steamed dumplings and festival skewers, carry towering stacks to the counter, collect the cash and build your tiny Ember Stall into a bustling lantern-lit restaurant. Hire crew, open new rooms, upgrade your cookers and keep the queue happy. Play alone or with up to four friends on one device with controllers.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No account.
