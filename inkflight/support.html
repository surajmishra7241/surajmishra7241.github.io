Inkflight Support

Need help? Email mail@shubhsandes.com or use Settings → Contact Support in the game. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your iPhone model and what happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap an arrow and it flies off the page in the direction it points. An arrow can't pass through another one, so free its way first. Three wrong taps and the page is lost. Clear every arrow to restore the picture underneath.

What do the tools do?

Pencil shows an arrow that can leave now, Eraser removes one arrow, Lens lights up every free arrow for a moment and Quill undoes your last move. Arrows with a gold dot are mirrors: tap the dot to turn them around.

What is the Atlas Club?

An optional monthly or yearly membership: no ads at all, a new members' page every day, a daily gift and instant video rewards. The yearly plan may start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Open it from the crown on the home screen, the Shop or Settings.

How do I cancel the Atlas Club?

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Inkflight, or tap Manage subscription on the Atlas Club screen. You keep the membership until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought something but don't have it.

Tap Restore Purchases in the Shop or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account. If it still doesn't appear, email us the date of the purchase.

Are there ads?

The game is free with ads: optional reward videos, a full-screen ad now and then between pages and a banner on later pages. No Ads turns off the full-screen ads and the banner for good; Atlas Club members see no ads at all.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device and in your iCloud (Settings → iCloud save). Reinstall the game or open it on another iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account and your pages, stars and coins come back. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases.

Are there leaderboards?

Yes. Sign in to Game Center and tap Settings → Game Center for the leaderboards and 13 achievements.

How do I get a refund?

Request it from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
