Inkflight — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers the game Inkflight (“the game”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”), on iPhone and iPad.

In short: the game is free. It shows ads (optional reward videos, an occasional full-screen ad between pages and a small banner while you play), served through Unity LevelPlay by ironSource Ads, Google AdMob and Unity Ads, and offers optional in-app purchases and an optional Atlas Club subscription, all processed by Apple. There are no accounts and no server of ours: your progress stays on your device. With usage stats on (the default, except that in the EEA, UK and Switzerland they wait for your answer on Google's consent form) anonymous gameplay statistics go to Google Analytics for Firebase; switch them off any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Ads are personalised only if usage stats are on and you allow tracking when Apple's prompt asks (and, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form allows it).

Information stored on your device

Your progress (pages finished, stars, coins, tools, pictures restored, daily gifts), items you bought, your membership status and your settings are saved in the game's own storage on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the game deletes it.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see how the game is played, improve it and measure our own advertising, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. When usage stats are on it receives:

  • an app-instance identifier created by Firebase and the identifier for vendors (IDFV), not your name, email or Apple ID;
  • device and app information: device model, iOS version, app version, language, country/region (derived from your IP address, which Google does not store as such), first-open and session times;
  • game events: pages started, won and lost, tools used, screens and offers viewed, purchases and subscriptions (product and price), reward videos watched and the estimated value of ads shown.

Google processes this under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and keeps detailed event data for 2 months (reports use aggregated counts).

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: nothing is collected until you answer Google's consent form, and only if you agree. The game treats you as there if your device's region is in one of these countries or Google's consent check says its form applies to you.
  • Everywhere: turn it off any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Stats then stop, ads are no longer personalised and the game does not ask for tracking.

Advertising

The game shows optional reward videos you choose to watch (for example, extra hearts, double coins, a free tool or free coins), a full-screen ad now and then as you leave a finished page (never while you are solving one, never after a lost page, and not on the first pages), and a small banner at the bottom of the puzzle screen on later pages. No Ads turns the full-screen ads and the banner off for good; Atlas Club members see no ads at all. Google AdMob ads are limited to its “G” content rating, and ad requests are not marked as directed at children.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies (ironSource), which picks each ad from ironSource Ads, Google AdMob and Unity Ads; LevelPlay includes Unity's Ad Quality component, which checks the ads shown for quality and safety. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, these ad partners may collect from your device: IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, the identifier for vendors (IDFV), ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostics. Each processes this under its own policy: Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, Unity Ads) and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. When usage stats are on, the game also sends the estimated value of each ad shown (no personal data) to Google Analytics for Firebase.

  • Tracking. If usage stats are on, the game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt once, after your first pages, with a short note first. Only if you choose Allow do the ad partners receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), may ads be personalised to your interests, and may Google Analytics share statistics with Google's advertising services to measure which of our ads brought you to the game. Either answer leaves the game the same; change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks for your ad choices before the first ad; change them later in Settings → Privacy Choices. The ad partners read your answers directly, and the game applies them to Google Analytics too.

Purchases and the Atlas Club subscription

Purchases (bundles, coins, Safety Net, No Ads) and the Atlas Club subscription are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. The App Store tells the game which items and which membership your Apple ID owns, with product, transaction identifiers and dates, so they can be delivered, renewed and restored; this stays on your device. You manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Apple services

The game may ask Apple to show its standard “rate this app” prompt after a good win. That prompt is run by Apple; we receive no information from it. Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated download, sales and crash statistics through App Store Connect if you have chosen to share them with developers.

Contacting us

If you email support from Settings → Contact Support, the draft includes the game version, your current page, your device model and iOS version so we can help. We receive your email address and anything you write only if you send it, use it only to answer you and delete the thread within 12 months.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health data or browsing history, and we do not sell personal information. If you allow tracking, our ad partners may use your advertising identifier for personalised ads, which some US state laws treat as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising; stop this any time by turning tracking off in iOS Settings or usage stats off in the game.

Children

Inkflight is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at mail@shubhsandes.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Switch usage stats off in Settings → Share usage stats, change ad choices in Settings → Privacy Choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland) or tracking in iOS Settings, or delete the game. We don't know who you are: analytics data is tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to a name or account. To ask Google to delete it, email us the approximate date you played and your device model and we will request deletion of that app instance. Email mail@shubhsandes.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
