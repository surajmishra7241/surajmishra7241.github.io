Spin & Carve — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Spin & Carve (“the game”), published by Suraj Mishra (“we”, “us”), handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the game on iPhone, iPad and Android.

In short: we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. Your progress is stored on your device (and in your own iCloud on iPhone). On iPhone and iPad the game sends anonymous play statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, which you can switch off in Settings → Share usage stats; nothing is sent for players under 13. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay by ironSource Ads, Google AdMob and Unity Ads. They are not personalised unless you are 16 or older and allow tracking when Apple's prompt asks. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, coins, settings, purchases you own and the age range you choose when the game asks (under 13, 13 to 15, or 16 or older) are saved on your device. Deleting the game deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time in Settings → Delete my data, which also clears the game's analytics identifier on the device.

iCloud (iPhone). If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so it comes back after a reinstall or on a new iPhone. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; we cannot see or access it. You can remove it by turning off iCloud for the game in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

There is no account, sign-up or login, and the game has no chat or way to share anything you type.

Your age

Before the first ad, the game asks how old you are. The answer stays on your device and is never shown to anyone. It decides:

Until you answer, the game sends no play statistics.

Play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase, iPhone and iPad)

To see how the game is played, fix levels that are too hard and learn which of our ads bring new players, the game sends play statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. The Android version does not send them.

  • What is sent: a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase; the identifier for vendors (IDFV); device model, iOS version, app version and language; your country or region (worked out from your IP address); when you open the game and for how long; and game events such as levels started and finished, stars earned, tutorial steps, coins earned and spent, ads offered and watched, and purchases (the product, its price and currency, never payment details).
  • What is not sent: your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or anything you type.
  • Your choice: turn it off at any time in Settings → Share usage stats; Firebase then collects nothing more. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it starts off and stays off until you turn it on.
  • Retention: we keep detailed event data in Google Analytics for no longer than 14 months.
  • Google processes this information under its own terms: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Privacy and security in Firebase.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay, Google AdMob, Unity Ads)

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies (ironSource), which picks each ad from ironSource Ads, Google AdMob and Unity Ads: an occasional full-screen ad after a finished piece, a small banner on some menu screens, and optional reward videos you choose to watch. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, these ad partners may collect from your device: IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, an app-specific identifier such as the identifier for vendors (IDFV) on iOS or the app set ID on Android, ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostics. Each processes this under its own policy: Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, Unity Ads) and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The game also sends the estimated value of each ad shown (no personal data) to Google Analytics for Firebase, when play statistics are on, so we can see which of our own ads bring players who enjoy the game.

  • By default ads are not personalised (no interest-based advertising) and are limited to the PG content rating (G for players under 13).
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad. If you said you are 16 or older, the game shows one short note, “About ads”, right after the age question and then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, before any ad loads. Only if you choose Allow, the ad partners above receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), ads may be personalised to your interests, and Google Analytics may share play statistics with Google's advertising services to measure which of our ads brought you to the game. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, nothing changes: ads stay non-personalised. The game is the same either way, and you can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players under 16 are never asked.
  • On Android the game does not personalise ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices when the game first starts; you can change them later in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Buying Remove Ads turns off the automatic ad breaks.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. The store tells the game which items your account owns so they can be delivered and restored (product, transaction identifiers and dates). Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health data or browsing history, and we do not sell personal information. We do not run our own server that receives your gameplay data; play statistics go to Google Analytics for Firebase as described above. If you allow tracking on iPhone or iPad, our ad partners may use your advertising identifier for personalised ads, which some US state laws treat as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can stop this at any time by turning tracking off in iOS Settings.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

Children

Spin & Carve is made for a general audience of all ages, so before the first ad it asks every player a neutral age question. If a player says they are under 13, the game sends no play statistics, never asks to track and shows only child-directed, non-personalised ads rated for all ages (G); for players under 16 ads are never personalised. Players under 13 can play the whole game this way; nothing about it is locked behind the age answer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at mail@shubhsandes.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. You can withdraw consent in the game at any time: switch off Share usage stats, change Ad privacy choices, or turn tracking off in iOS Settings. Your game data stays on your device, so you can delete it yourself with Settings → Delete my data or by deleting the game. For anything else, including deleting play statistics already sent to Google Analytics, email mail@shubhsandes.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Security and retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete it or the game. Play statistics are kept as described above. Network traffic from the game uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the date above, and, for significant changes, tell you in the game before the change applies.
